# Plinkified EQL Command Center

The goal is to create the ultimate desktop companion for EverQuest Legacy.

The application should allow the player to understand their adventure at a glance.

It should answer questions like:

- How am I doing?
- Where have I been?
- What happened today?
- What dropped?
- What killed me?
- Where did I spend my time?
- How much DPS am I doing?
- How efficient is this camp?

The application should remain simple to use while exposing deep information when requested.

Every feature should support the philosophy:

"Most important information in two seconds."

The UI should be clean, resizable, customizable, and approachable for older gamers.
